Trust & Safety

FOMO NOMO only works if it feels safe to show up. Let's follow these rules to keep it that way:

  • Community Guidelines
  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms of Service

Verified campus communities

Every feed is tied to a verified school email (like @ucf.edu). You'll only ever see your own campus's events and posts. No outside accounts or noise from other campuses.

Report anything, instantly

All posts, comments, and profiles have a Report option built right in. If something's off, flag it and it will be reviewed.

Block whoever you want

  • Quick Blocking

    Tap the 3-dot menu on any post or comment to immediately block a user. They will no longer see your content or be able to comment.

  • Manage Attendees

    Hosting? Open the 'See Attendees' list to manage your guests. You have two options: "Remove" to simply take someone off the list, or "Block" to permanently ensure they can never rejoin your event.

We actually moderate

Reports don't go into the void. Content that breaks our Community Guidelines gets removed, and repeat or serious violations lead to suspension or a permanent ban.

Safety first

A few basics: meet in public, well-lit places, tell a friend where you're headed, and trust your gut. FOMO NOMO helps you find what's happening – the same common sense you'd use meeting anyone new still applies.

Something feel wrong? Reach us anytime at support@fomonomoapp.com.
