Meet the Developer

Hello, I'm the student developer behind FOMO NOMO!

My inspiration to make this app came from seeing all the community engagement from student-hosted events like milk-maniac chugging milk, garage bands, performative male, and meowing contests. I wanted to build a platform where you never miss out on the fun and memorable stuff happening right around you.

Shoutout to @liampatryan and @nick_patone.

If you enjoy the app and want to help support, leaving a review on the app store or sharing it with a friend is greatly appreciated!
